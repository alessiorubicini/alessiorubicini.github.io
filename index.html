Create human-centered AI experiences in SwiftUI. This package mirrors Apple Intelligence’s design language, animations, and interaction patterns to make AI features feel intuitive and perfectly at home on Apple devices.

A robust framework for safe, structured communication in concurrent Swift systems. Implements binary session types to ensure reliable interactions. Built as my Bachelor’s thesis project.

Pencil Drawer for SwiftUI

Integrate drawing into your apps effortlessly. Pencil Drawer simplifies PencilKit usage with SwiftUI bindings, letting users draw smoothly and interactively with minimal setup.
