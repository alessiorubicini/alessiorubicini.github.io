The Manifesto

I built Fynn out of frustration. Every expense tracker I tried felt like it was designed for an accountant or a stock trader, rather than a regular person trying to keep an eye on their budget. They were cluttered with complex charts, confusing categorization systems, and ugly interfaces that felt like a starship control panel. I just wanted a simple, elegant way to write down what I spent, without the app demanding my attention or making me feel financially inadequate. I couldn't find a tool that completely respected my time and felt calm to use, so I decided to build one myself. Fynn is designed around exactly two things: intentionality and subtraction. It leaves out the noise and focuses entirely on the essentials, letting you track your money gracefully, privately, and beautifully. It doesn't tell you how to live your life, it just helps you see your money with clarity.
