Automating Fynn with Apple Shortcuts

Fynn was built to respect your time. By integrating deeply with Apple Shortcuts, you can log expenses, incomes, and transfers without ever opening the app. Fynn provides three powerful actions inside the Shortcuts app. You can use these to build custom automations, ask Siri to log transactions, or link them to your Apple Pay.

  • Add Expense

    Logs a new expense. You just need to provide the Amount, the Merchant name, and choose which Fynn Account to debit. You can also optionally assign a Category and add Notes.

  • Add Income

    Registers new incoming money. Requires the Amount, the Source (where the money came from), and the destination Account. Like expenses, categories and notes are optional.

  • Add Transfer

    Moves money between your existing Fynn accounts. Requires the Amount, the From Account, and the To Account. Fynn handles the double-entry bookkeeping automatically for you.

Tutorial: Automate Apple Pay Expenses

One of the best ways to use Fynn is to have it automatically log an expense every time you tap your phone to pay. Because Fynn runs silently in the background, your Apple Pay experience stays fast and seamless.

How to set it up:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap on the Automation tab at the bottom, then tap the + button in the top right.
  3. Scroll down and select Wallet ("When I tap a Wallet Card or Pass").
  4. Choose the cards and categories you want to trigger this automation, make sure "Run Immediately" is checked, and tap Next.
  5. Select New Blank Automation.
  6. Open the bottom sheet, search for Fynn, and select the Add Expense action.
  7. Now, map the shortcut data to Fynn:
    • Tap Amount, select Shortcut Input, and choose Payment Amount.
    • Tap Merchant, select Shortcut Input, and choose Merchant.
    • Tap Account and pick the Fynn account you want the money to leave from.
    💡 Tip: You can also leave these fields empty. If you do, Fynn will automatically prompt you to enter them when the shortcut runs.
  8. Tap Done. You're all set! Next time you pay with your iPhone, Fynn will magically log the transaction in the background without needing any confirmation.

Tutorial: iPhone Back Tap

For even faster logging, you can configure your iPhone to run a Fynn shortcut just by double-tapping or triple-tapping the back of your phone.

How to set it up:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and create a new shortcut using one of the Fynn actions. Save it so it appears in your Shortcuts library.
  2. Open your iPhone's Settings app.
  3. Navigate to Accessibility > Touch > Back Tap (at the very bottom).
  4. Choose either Double Tap or Triple Tap.
  5. Scroll down to the Shortcuts section and select the shortcut you just created.
  6. That's it! Now just tap the back of your iPhone to instantly log a transaction from anywhere.
